Neural Cellular Automata

Every cell runs the same tiny neural network on its 3×3 neighbourhood. Click and drag on the canvas to damage the pattern; the cells repair it.

Runs entirely in your browser (plain JavaScript on the CPU, no server). The weights are exported from the PyTorch models in this repository, and a test checks that this runner reproduces the PyTorch rollout numerically. Emoji targets are from Noto Emoji (Apache-2.0).
